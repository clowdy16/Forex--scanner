Choose your pairs and hit Scan to pull data.
FX data: daily ECB reference rates via the free Frankfurter API (frankfurter.app). Gold (XAU/USD): Yahoo Finance's public chart endpoint. Both are end-of-day/delayed, not intraday ticks — and if the page can't reach them (e.g. a sandboxed preview with no outbound network access), it shows simulated data instead and says so above the table. Signals are simple rule-based reads on SMA20/50, RSI14, MACD(12,26,9) and Bollinger(20,2) — not trading advice.
